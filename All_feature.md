This Project Development Agreement is entered into between SequenceIT and Kazi Rana. It defines the scope, commercial terms, responsibilities, delivery conditions, and other terms governing development of the Unified Marketplace Dashboard and AI-powered Market Research Bot. 

### **1. Project Purpose** 

SequenceIT will design, develop, integrate, test, and deploy a centralized e-commerce management platform intended to streamline multi-channel operations across supported marketplaces. The platform will centralize product management, inventory, orders, customer communications, analytics, automation, and AI-assisted market research.

### **3. AI Market Research Bot Module** 

The project includes a Market Research Bot designed to collect, aggregate, and analyze marketplace data to support product and market decisions. 

- AI market analysis 

- AI product recommendations 

- AI-generated research reports 

- Collection of relevant marketplace metrics such as sales volume, search ranking, pricing, and customer reviews 

- Analysis of trends, potential profit margins, and product opportunity scores 

- Presentation of selected opportunities within the Research Dashboard 

- Ability to add selected opportunities to the seller product catalog 

**Research accuracy target:** up to 85%, subject to source-data quality, marketplace limitations, API availability, and AI/model behavior. This target is not a guarantee of business outcomes.
